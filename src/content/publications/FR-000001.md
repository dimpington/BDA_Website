## Purpose

This Field Report records an examination of the surviving promotional website created for the 1996 motion picture *Space Jam*. The examination was undertaken to document the site's continued public accessibility, identify characteristics of preservation interest, and assess its status as an operational legacy Digital Object.

The site remains accessible at its established network location and retains substantial elements of its original presentation and navigational structure.

## Object Examined

**FWX Reference:** FWX-000002  
**Object:** Space Jam Website  
**Object Type:** Website  
**Original Publication:** 1996  
**Preservation Status:** In Situ  
**Operational Status:** Active Legacy Site

The examined object was originally published as a promotional website associated with the theatrical release of *Space Jam*. Its structure reflects conventions common to commercial World Wide Web publishing during the mid-1990s, including image-based navigation, patterned backgrounds, discrete themed sections, and a comparatively shallow information architecture.

Unlike a reconstructed or emulated historical website, the examined object remains available as a functioning network resource.

## Field Examination

Access to the object was established through a contemporary web browser at its recorded public location.

The primary navigational interface remains operational and presents the visitor with a central graphical arrangement linking to multiple themed areas of the site. These areas retain visual characteristics strongly associated with the period of original publication.

No attempt was made during this examination to modify, reproduce, or otherwise intervene in the object.

The significance of the site lies not solely in the survival of its individual files, images, or written material. Its continued availability at a public network location preserves a form of interaction with the historical Web that cannot be entirely represented by screenshots or isolated archival captures.

A visitor is not merely viewing a representation of a website formerly available in 1996. The visitor is navigating a surviving descendant of that website through the same fundamental medium for which it was created.

## Preservation Assessment

FWX-000002 meets the Bureau criteria for continued observation as an **Active Legacy Site**.

The object possesses particular preservation value due to the combination of:

- continued public accessibility;
- identifiable mid-1990s web design characteristics;
- association with a major commercial media property;
- retention of historically characteristic navigational and visual elements; and
- continued operation as a network-accessible website rather than solely as an archived reproduction.

In accordance with the principles established in **PB-000001 — Continued Accessibility of Active Legacy Websites**, no intervention is presently recommended.

Preservation should remain **in situ** for as long as the original object continues to be publicly accessible and sufficiently representative of its historical form.

## Observations

The examination illustrates a recurring difficulty in the preservation of networked Digital Objects.

A surviving website is simultaneously historical material and an active system.

Its continued operation may introduce changes resulting from hosting infrastructure, browser behavior, network dependencies, or maintenance undertaken by its custodian. Absolute technical immutability therefore cannot be assumed.

These changes do not necessarily invalidate the object's historical significance.

For the purposes of the Bureau catalogue, continued observation and periodic verification are considered preferable to replacement of the surviving object with a Bureau-controlled reproduction.

Should the original network location cease operation, undergo substantial alteration, or become inaccessible for an extended period, its preservation status should be reassessed.

## Disposition

**Classification:** Historically Significant Digital Object  
**Recommended Action:** Continued Observation  
**Preservation Recommendation:** Maintain In Situ designation  
**Catalogue Action:** Retain FWX-000002 as an Active Legacy Site

No recovery or intervention action is required at this time.
