## Purpose

This Preservation Bulletin defines the Bureau's approach to historically
significant Digital Objects that remain publicly accessible at their original
network locations.

## Preservation Position

Where a Digital Object remains operational, publicly accessible and
historically representative, the Bureau may designate its preservation status
as **In Situ**.

In situ preservation prioritises continued observation, documentation and
verification of the original Digital Object rather than immediate duplication
or reconstruction.

## Monitoring Requirements

Active legacy websites designated for in situ preservation should be subject to
routine observation.

Monitoring may include:

- accessibility verification
- integrity assessment
- documentation of significant changes
- confirmation of current network location
- review of external archival copies
