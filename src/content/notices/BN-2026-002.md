The Bureau of Digital Antiquities announces the establishment of a continuing
Digital Preservation Review Programme for Digital Objects recorded in the
Forgotten Web Exchange.

Under the programme, designated Bureau offices will periodically review
catalogued Digital Objects to verify their accessibility, integrity and current
preservation status. Review findings will be incorporated into the relevant
FWX catalogue records as part of the Bureau's continuing preservation and
documentation activities.

The programme also provides for the identification of Digital Objects whose
original network locations have become unavailable, materially altered or
unrelated to the historically documented object.

Review frequency may vary according to operational status, preservation
condition and the availability of surviving network or archival resources.
