The Bureau of Digital Antiquities announces the commencement of public access
to its principal digital records, publications and research services.

The Bureau's public information system has been established to provide a stable
point of access to material concerning the identification, documentation and
preservation of historically significant Digital Objects. The service brings
together institutional records, preservation documentation, research
publications and information concerning the work of the Bureau and its
constituent offices and divisions.

Central to the service is the **Forgotten Web Exchange (FWX)**, the Bureau's
public catalogue of documented Digital Objects. FWX records provide structured
information concerning historical addresses, operational status, preservation
condition, provenance and surviving or reconstructed access points where these
can be established.

Initial public holdings represent a limited portion of the Bureau's documented
material. Additional Digital Object Records, collections, publications and
preservation assessments will be released as documentary review and cataloguing
work is completed.

The establishment of public access does not mark the completion of the Bureau's
digital preservation programme. Records may be amended as new evidence is
recovered, provenance is clarified or the preservation status of a Digital
Object changes. Where appropriate, superseded information will be retained as
part of the documentary record.

The Bureau will continue to identify endangered digital material, conduct
preservation and recovery work, maintain documentary records and make
historically significant digital heritage available for public study wherever
legal, technical and preservation conditions permit.

Public access is available through the Bureau's official digital service at
**digitalantiquities.org**.
