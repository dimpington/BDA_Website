# Overview

The Heaven's Gate website formed part of the public Internet presence of the
Heaven's Gate religious movement during the 1990s.

By March 1997, the website was being used to publish the group's writings and
other material while members of the movement also distributed messages through
Usenet and other Internet channels.

Following the end of the group in 1997, the website remained online under the
stewardship of former members. Its continued availability at the historical
domain makes it an unusual surviving example of a 1990s website whose
significance derives both from its original content and from the continuity of
its public accessibility.

The Bureau monitors the object in situ. The present record documents the
continued accessibility and legacy presentation of the website without
asserting that every element remains technically identical to its 1997 state.
