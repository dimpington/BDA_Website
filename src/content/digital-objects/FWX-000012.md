# Overview

FogCam is a networked imaging service established at San Francisco State
University in 1994 by students Jeff Schwartz and Dan Wong.

The project used a camera, computer equipment, and custom software to
periodically capture images from the university campus and make them
available through the World Wide Web.

FogCam followed the emergence of networked camera systems such as the
Trojan Room Coffee Pot at the University of Cambridge. Although it was not
the first webcam, its continued operation after earlier systems ceased made
FogCam an unusually persistent example of early networked imaging.

Over its operating life the camera has occupied several locations and
presented different views of the San Francisco State University campus.
The service continues to publish periodically updated images rather than
continuous streaming video.

# Institutional Stewardship

In August 2019, Schwartz and Wong announced their intention to discontinue
FogCam after approximately twenty-five years of operation.

The proposed closure attracted substantial public and media attention.
San Francisco State University subsequently agreed to assume responsibility
for maintaining the service, and operational control was transferred to the
university.

FogCam is presently maintained by San Francisco State University's Academic
Technology organization.

For preservation purposes, the Bureau considers this transfer significant.
The continued availability of FogCam represents active institutional
stewardship of a historical networked service rather than merely the
survival of an abandoned website.

# Preservation Note

FogCam remains publicly accessible as an operating networked imaging
service.

Continuity of the Digital Object does not imply that every physical or
technical component remains identical to those deployed in 1994. Cameras,
computing equipment, software, network infrastructure, and physical
locations may change while the historically continuous service persists.

The Bureau therefore records FogCam as an active legacy service maintained
under institutional stewardship.

Its relationship to the earlier Trojan Room Coffee Pot is historically
significant. FogCam's operators acknowledged the earlier Cambridge webcam,
and the FogCam camera was at times directed toward a campus coffee-shop
queue in tribute to the Trojan Room project.
