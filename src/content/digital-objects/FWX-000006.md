# Overview

Yahoo! Directory originated in 1994 as Jerry and David's Guide to the World
Wide Web, a project created by Stanford University graduate students Jerry
Yang and David Filo.

Rather than maintaining a machine-generated index of webpages, the service
organized web resources within a browsable hierarchy of subjects and
subcategories. This structure provided users with a means of navigating the
rapidly expanding World Wide Web through human-organized classification.

The project developed into Yahoo!, and the directory remained a distinct
component of the wider Yahoo service as the company expanded into search,
communications, news, media, and other Internet services.

Yahoo! Directory remained operational for approximately two decades. Yahoo
announced its discontinuation in 2014, and the service closed on
31 December 2014.

For catalogue purposes, the Bureau treats Yahoo! Directory as a distinct
Digital Object separate from the wider Yahoo web portal, Yahoo Search, and
the corporate entities that have operated under the Yahoo name.

# Preservation Note

The original directory service is no longer operational. Its historical
structure and contents survive through archived webpages, contemporary
documentation, and other historical records.

The Bureau records the object as an archival preservation case. Particular
historical states of the directory may differ between surviving captures,
and no currently operating Yahoo service is considered a continuation of
the Digital Object represented by this record.
