## Overview

The early commercial web represented a period of rapid experimentation,
creative design and emerging online business models.

This collection documents Digital Objects that illustrate how commercial
organisations, advertisers and media companies adapted to the growing
World Wide Web during the late 1990s and early 2000s.

## Curatorial Note

Rather than preserving isolated websites, this collection examines them
as part of a broader historical movement that shaped the modern Internet.
