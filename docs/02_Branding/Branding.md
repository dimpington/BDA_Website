# Bureau of Digital Antiquities

## Design Philosophy

The Bureau should appear as a legitimate institution.

The visual identity should communicate:

- Trust
- Preservation
- Research
- History
- Government
- Archival Quality

The website should not feel retro for the sake of nostalgia.

Instead, it should feel like an official government website that has evolved slowly over decades while preserving its identity.

### FWX Digital Object Preview Images

Public Digital Object records may include a standardized preview image providing a visual reference for the catalogued object.

Preview images shall follow the following convention:

- **Dimensions:** 1600 × 900 pixels
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Format:** WebP
- **Directory:** `public/images/fwx/`
- **Filename:** `fwx-[six-digit identifier].webp`
- **Example:** `fwx-000001.webp`

Preview images are descriptive catalogue assets intended to provide a representative visual reference to a Digital Object. They do not constitute preservation copies or authoritative archival captures.

Where the original Digital Object does not naturally conform to a 16:9 aspect ratio, the preview should be cropped or framed without distortion to provide a representative view.
