# BDA Personnel Register

**Document status:** Internal reference  
**Purpose:** Canonical register of recurring Bureau personnel, positions,
characterisation, naming provenance and usage guidance.

> This document is a development reference and is not intended for direct
> publication. Naming-provenance sections describe out-of-universe references
> and Easter eggs.

## Office of the Director

### Dr. Theodore V. Elson

**Position:** Director  
**Unit:** Bureau of Digital Antiquities  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Senior institutional head of the Bureau. Scholarly rather than political,
restrained in public writing, and strongly committed to treating digital
material as legitimate cultural heritage.

His name may appear on major Bureau Notices, annual reports, institutional
directives and formal correspondence.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** Ted Nelson

The name is an Easter egg referencing Ted Nelson, who coined the term
"hypertext" and conceived Project Xanadu.

"Theodore" provides the connection to Ted, while "V. Elson" obscures Nelson
enough that the reference is not immediately apparent.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Major Bureau Notices
- Annual reports
- Institutional directives
- Formal correspondence
- Forewords and statements from the Bureau

Generally avoid:
- Routine Digital Object Records
- Technical forensic findings
- Field Reports

## Office of Digital Preservation

### Dr. Evelyn Rothen

**Position:** Chief Preservation Officer  
**Unit:** Office of Digital Preservation  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Methodical and academically minded. The Bureau's senior authority on
long-term preservation strategy, format longevity, integrity policy and
institutional preservation planning.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** Jeff Rothenberg

Rothenberg was an influential contributor to discussions surrounding
digital preservation and technological obsolescence. "Rothen" is a
deliberately shortened form of the surname.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Preservation policy
- Preservation assessments
- Technical preservation guidance
- Formal review or approval of preservation work

## Bureau Archives

### Warren B. Kale

**Position:** Chief Archivist  
**Unit:** Bureau Archives  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Long-serving institutional archivist responsible for accessioned records,
historical holdings and preservation copies. Quietly possesses an alarming
knowledge of where almost everything in the Bureau is stored.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** Brewster Kahle

Founder of the Internet Archive. "B. Kale" is a deliberately obscured
rendering of B. Kahle.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Archival accession records
- Historical holdings
- Collection documentation
- Archival correspondence

## Publications Office

### Robert C. Lieu

**Position:** Director of Publications  
**Unit:** Publications Office  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Oversees Bureau reports, technical notes, editorial standards and publication
consistency. Formal and exacting, particularly regarding Bureau terminology.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** Robert Cailliau

Cailliau was an important figure in the early development and promotion of
the World Wide Web at CERN. "Robert" remains as the principal breadcrumb,
while "C. Lieu" loosely disguises Cailliau.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Publication approvals
- Editorial notes
- Annual reports
- Publication policy

## Digital Forensics Unit

### Clive R. Stollard

**Position:** Chief Digital Forensics Officer  
**Unit:** Digital Forensics Unit  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Technically formidable and skeptical of assumptions. Responsible for
forensic examination of recovered systems and media, with a strong emphasis
on separating observed evidence from interpretation.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** Clifford Stoll

Stoll documented his investigation of an early computer intrusion in
*The Cuckoo's Egg*. "Clifford Stoll" becomes the less conspicuous
"Clive Stollard."

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Forensic assessments
- Recovered-media examinations
- Integrity investigations
- Technical findings

## Recovery Operations Division

### Peter A. Deutschmann

**Position:** Director of Recovery Operations  
**Unit:** Recovery Operations Division  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Practical rather than academic. Responsible for locating endangered Digital
Objects, coordinating recovery operations and determining when preservation
work requires direct intervention.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** Peter Deutsch / Archie

Peter Deutsch was one of the creators of Archie, an early Internet search
system used to locate files on FTP servers.

The reference is particularly appropriate to Recovery Operations: both
involve locating digital material distributed across networks.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Recovery authorisations
- Operational directives
- Field assignments
- Recovery assessments
- Review of Field Reports

### A. V. Waymark

**Position:** Senior Field Officer  
**Unit:** Recovery Operations Division  
**Status:** Active  
**Reports to:** Director of Recovery Operations

#### Character

Seasoned Bureau field officer and one of its experienced practical Internet
archaeologists.

Waymark is typically assigned when provenance is uncertain, infrastructure
is obsolete, ownership is unclear, or a Digital Object may represent a
last-known surviving installation.

#### Naming provenance

**Reference:** PINNED — original Easter egg to be recovered.

Do not invent or replace the original explanation. The name was deliberately
created as an Easter egg during development and its original reference should
be documented when recovered.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Field Reports
- Recovery assessments
- Site examinations
- Operational annotations

Generally avoid:
- Routine Bureau administration
- Institutional policy statements

### Veronica G. Archer

**Position:** Bulletin Editor  
**Unit:** Preservation Bulletin Service  
**Status:** Active

#### Character

Responsible for recurring preservation advisories and operational bulletins.
Her writing is concise, procedural and designed primarily to make information
easy to discover and act upon.

#### Naming provenance

**References:** Veronica, Gopher and Archie

Veronica was an early search system associated with Gopher. The middle
initial "G." provides a second reference to Gopher, while "Archer" subtly
echoes Archie.

#### Usage

Appropriate:
- Preservation Bulletins
- Operational advisories
- Bulletin corrections
- Recurring preservation guidance

# Personnel usage principles

Names should appear naturally and sparingly. BDA personnel are primarily
institutional actors rather than characters presented directly to the reader.

Repeated names across reports, notices, records and correspondence should
gradually imply a functioning organisation without requiring explicit
biographical pages.

Easter eggs should never compromise the institutional tone of the Bureau.
A name must remain plausible even when its underlying reference is not
recognised.

Personnel positions and names in this register are canonical unless this
document is deliberately revised.
