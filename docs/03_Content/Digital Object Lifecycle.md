# Digital Object Lifecycle

## Overview

Digital Objects progress through the Bureau according to operational requirements.

Not every discovered object enters the Bureau's permanent archive.

Qualification is determined following an initial assessment and only qualified objects continue through the preservation lifecycle.

## Lifecycle Stages

### Discovery

A potential Digital Object is identified through research, public submissions, archival partners or other sources.

---

### Initial Assessment

The Bureau performs a preliminary review to determine whether further investigation is justified.

---

### Qualification Decision

Following initial assessment, the Bureau determines whether the object falls within its preservation scope.

Objects that do not satisfy Bureau criteria are closed without further investigation.

---

### Investigation

Qualified Digital Objects undergo historical, technical and provenance investigation.

Multiple departments may participate depending on operational requirements.

---

### Preservation

The Bureau determines what information or material should be preserved.

Not every Digital Object requires active preservation.

---

### Cataloguing

A permanent FWX record is created and associated metadata is recorded.

---

### Publication

Where appropriate, official Bureau publications are issued.

Publication is not mandatory for every Digital Object.

---

### Monitoring

Significant Digital Objects may be periodically reviewed to verify operational status and preservation state.

---

### Reassessment

Digital Objects may be reassessed when new information, recovered material or historical context becomes available.
