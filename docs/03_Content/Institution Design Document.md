# Bureau Departments

---

## Office of Digital Preservation
"Ensuring continued accessibility."

Establishes preservation standards and oversees the long-term stewardship of Digital Objects.

The Office develops Bureau preservation policy, maintains archival standards and ensures that preserved Digital Objects remain accessible for future study.

Primary Responsibilities

- Preservation policy and standards
- Long-term accessibility monitoring
- Integrity verification programmes
- Preservation assessments
- Oversight of Digital Object stewardship

---

## Records & Provenance Division
"Authenticity through documentation."

Documents the origin, ownership, historical context and provenance of accepted Digital Objects.

The Division maintains historical documentation supporting authenticity and long-term archival value.

Primary Responsibilities

- Provenance research
- Ownership documentation
- Historical context
- Archival citations
- Authenticity documentation

---

## Recovery Operations Division
"No Digital Object permanently lost."

Conducts archival recovery of Digital Objects that are partially or wholly unavailable.

Recovery activities may include archival reconstruction, media acquisition and recovery from historical Internet archives or physical media.

Primary Responsibilities

- Archival recovery
- Reconstruction of incomplete Digital Objects
- Acquisition of historical media
- External archive coordination
- Recovery case documentation

---

## Digital Forensics Unit
"Verification through evidence."

Verifies authenticity, integrity and technical characteristics of Digital Objects.

The Unit performs technical examinations and documents evidence supporting authenticity, preservation and provenance.

Primary Responsibilities

- Authenticity verification
- Technical analysis
- Integrity assessment
- Metadata examination
- Digital evidence documentation

---

## Publications Office
"Knowledge through publication."

Produces official Bureau publications for public dissemination.

The Office is responsible for Field Reports, Preservation Bulletins, Technical Notes and Annual Reports.

Primary Responsibilities

- Field Reports
- Preservation Bulletins
- Technical Notes
- Annual Reports
- Public guidance

---

## Bureau Archives

Maintains the Bureau's permanent institutional archives and historical records.

The Bureau Archives preserve official Bureau documentation, historical publications, organisational records and institutional history for long-term public reference.

Primary Responsibilities:

- Permanent Bureau records
- Institutional history
- Historical publications
- Organisational archives
- Public archival access
