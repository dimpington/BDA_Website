# Bureau of Digital Antiquities Lexicon

This document defines preferred institutional terminology used across Bureau
records, publications, interfaces and public information systems.

## Bureau of Digital Antiquities

The public institution responsible for the identification, documentation,
preservation and study of historically significant Digital Objects.

Preferred short form: **the Bureau**.

## Digital Object

A historically significant digital entity formally documented or preserved by
the Bureau.

Digital Objects may include websites, software, network services, digital
communities, documents, media, software environments and related digital
artefacts.

Preferred capitalization: **Digital Object**.

## Forgotten Web Exchange

The Bureau's central archival and records management system.

Preferred short form: **FWX**.

FWX stores and manages Digital Object Records, preservation information and
associated institutional records.

## Digital Object Record

The formal FWX record associated with a Digital Object.

A Digital Object Record documents the identity, provenance, status,
preservation condition and related institutional information of a Digital
Object.

Preferred capitalization: **Digital Object Record**.

## FWX Record

Permitted short form for **Digital Object Record** where the FWX context is
already clear.

## Catalogue

The public-facing collection of approved FWX records made available through the
Bureau website.

Preferred wording: **FWX Catalogue** or **public catalogue**.

## Preservation Status

The Bureau's recorded assessment of the preservation condition or preservation
method associated with a Digital Object.

Preferred capitalization in formal field labels: **Preservation Status**.

## Operational Status

The recorded operational condition of a Digital Object or service.

Examples include Active, Active Legacy Site and Defunct.

## Accessibility

The recorded means by which a Digital Object, reconstruction, documentation or
archival representation remains available for study.

## Integrity Status

The Bureau's assessment of the condition, completeness or authenticity of the
available Digital Object or surviving archival record.

## Preservation Assessment

A Bureau assessment documenting accessibility, integrity, monitoring activity
and preservation status for a Digital Object.

## Collection

A curated grouping of Digital Objects assembled around a shared historical,
technical or thematic context.

Preferred capitalization when referring to a formal Bureau entity:
**Collection**.

## Publication

An official Bureau document issued independently of a Digital Object Record.

Publications may reference Digital Objects but do not replace their FWX records.

Examples include Field Reports, Preservation Bulletins, Technical Notes and
Annual Reports.

## Bureau Notice

An official institutional notice issued by the Bureau.

Preferred capitalization: **Bureau Notice**.

## Historical Address

The documented network address historically associated with a Digital Object.

A Historical Address does not imply that the present-day destination remains
associated with the original Digital Object.

## Current Address

A network address currently associated with or providing access to the
catalogued Digital Object, reconstruction or preserved representation.

## In Situ Preservation

Preservation of a Digital Object while it remains accessible at an active or
continuing network location.

## Documentary Preservation

Preservation through records, documentation, technical evidence and other
surviving information where the original Digital Object is no longer
operational or directly accessible.
