# Bureau Organization

---

## Organizational Model

The Bureau of Digital Antiquities is organized as a small specialized public institution dedicated to the preservation of historically significant digital heritage.

All operational departments report directly to the Director.

```text
Director
│
├── Office of Digital Preservation
├── Records & Provenance Division
├── Recovery Operations Division
├── Digital Forensics Unit
└── Publications Office
```

---

## Organizational Principles

The Bureau operates through specialized departments with clearly defined responsibilities.

Digital Objects are assigned to departments according to operational requirements rather than a fixed procedural sequence.

The Forgotten Web Exchange (FWX) serves as the Bureau's central archival and records management system and supports all operational departments.

---

## Organizational Decisions

### OD-001 — Bureau Structure

The Bureau consists of five operational departments reporting directly to the Director.

**Status:** Accepted

---

### OD-002 — FWX Classification

The Forgotten Web Exchange (FWX) is an institutional archival and records management system rather than an operational department.

**Status:** Accepted

---

### OD-003 — Operational Workflow

Digital Objects follow workflows appropriate to their preservation requirements rather than a fixed departmental sequence.

**Status:** Accepted
