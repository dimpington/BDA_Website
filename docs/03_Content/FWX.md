# Forgotten Web Exchange (FWX)

---

## Overview

The Forgotten Web Exchange (FWX) is the Bureau of Digital Antiquities' central archival and records management system.

FWX stores, indexes and manages information relating to Digital Objects, preservation activities, publications and associated archival records.

FWX supports all operational departments within the Bureau.

---

## Public Access

Selected FWX records are made available through the Bureau's public website.

Public records may include preservation summaries, Digital Object metadata, associated publications and approved media.

---

## Internal Access

Internal FWX records contain operational information intended exclusively for Bureau personnel.

Internal records may include research notes, provenance documentation, preservation assessments, technical examinations and administrative records.

---

## Purpose

FWX serves as the Bureau's institutional memory.

It provides a unified system for documenting Digital Objects throughout their lifecycle and preserves the relationships between Digital Objects, publications, assessments and archival activities.
